MONTE MONTEPARE

Monte Montepare is a comedian, storyteller, and adventure guide originally from Colorado. He has been the keynote speaker at the Ouray Ice Festival, appears on the cover of The Alaska Packraft Guidebook, and is part owner of Kennicott Wilderness Guides in Wrangell–St. Elias National Park, Alaska.

Monte is a Moth GrandSLAM Champion and has toured extensively with The Moth on its MainStage. His stories have been featured twice on The Moth Radio Hour, and he was featured in How to Tell a Story: The Essential Guide to Memorable Storytelling from The Moth. His story “Red Flags and Blue Ice” was The Moth’s most-shared story of 2024, and his story “Nowhere to Run” appears in A Point of Beauty: True Stories of Holding On and Letting Go.

He also has extensive experience in improvisational comedy, including five years on a house team at the Upright Citizens Brigade. He continues to perform regularly with his indie improv team, Rat King, whose variety show runs the second and fourth Tuesday of every month.

Monte has worked as a ghostwriter, story coach, and director, helping people find, shape, and tell their stories. He has also MC’d events including the American Mountain Guides Association Annual Meeting, Ouray Ice Festival, Bishop Craggin’ Classic, Mountainfilm and more!

Whether he’s telling stories, making people laugh, hosting a room, or helping someone else find the story they need to tell, Monte brings a combination of professional stage experience, improvisational instincts, and more than two decades of experience in the world of adventure and exploration.

He lives in a little house on a hill with his partner, their great and powerful daughter, and a magical chiweenie named Sage.
